Does testable science survive its own incentives?

Researchers either publish a risky prediction or a flexible story that can't fail. Set the rewards and watch which habit spreads.

A toy model, not a measurement. The rewards are the paper's illustrative numbers, and the paper is AI-written with no recorded human review. The page shows what the maths implies, not what science is doing.

Limits

  • Two strategies, fixed payoffs, no networks, no differences between researchers. The paper lists these as open questions.
  • The central prediction (a higher b raises the share of registered predictions) is untested. Technology readiness level 2.
  • A rise in b could also change s or c in real life, which would confound any pilot.
